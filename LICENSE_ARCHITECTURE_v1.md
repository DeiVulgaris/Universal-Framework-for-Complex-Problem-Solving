UFCPS License Architecture v1

Status

Draft architectural specification.
This document defines the intended separation between Metamonism, the UFCPS protocol, and UFCPS software. It is not a final legal license.

1. Purpose

UFCPS is an open research and technical framework for distributed process continuity, recursion, reflection, and further investigation of reflexive process discrimination.

The licensing architecture should preserve two principles:

UFCPS should remain freely available for research, experimentation, implementation, and development.

The ontological origin of the framework should remain explicitly attributed to Metamonism and its author.

No royalty or other mandatory economic payment is part of this licensing architecture.

2. Three-Layer Structure

UFCPS distinguishes three related but different objects.

METAMONISM
    │
    │ ontological foundation
    ▼
UFCPS
    │
    │ technical protocol / specifications
    ▼
IMPLEMENTATIONS
    │
    │ software and experimental systems
    ▼
UFCPS-BASED SYSTEMS

These layers should not be conflated in the final licensing documents.

3. Metamonism

3.1 Role

Metamonism is the ontological foundation of UFCPS.

The central ontological principle underlying the framework is the Prohibition of Indifference (¬∅).

UFCPS does not claim that its technical protocol constitutes Metamonism itself. Rather, UFCPS is a technical research architecture developed from the Metamonist ontological framework.

3.2 Attribution

The canonical attribution should be:

Metamonism — ontological foundation of UFCPS.

Extended form:

UFCPS is based on the Metamonist ontological framework developed by Andrii Myshko.

Where appropriate, publications and implementations should preserve this attribution.

3.3 Scope

The attribution requirement concerns the documented intellectual origin and conceptual foundation of UFCPS.

This document does not attempt to establish exclusive legal ownership over abstract philosophical ideas, mathematical concepts, scientific principles, or methods as such.

The final legal instruments must distinguish between:

protected textual expression;

software;

specifications and documentation;

names and marks;

abstract ideas and principles.

4. UFCPS Protocol

UFCPS is the technical protocol and research architecture derived from the above ontological foundation.

The intended licensing policy is:

free access;

free research use;

free experimentation;

free implementation;

free modification;

free academic and non-commercial use;

commercial use permitted under the selected final protocol license, unless a later version explicitly states otherwise.

The protocol should not require payment merely because a person or organization:

reads UFCPS;

studies UFCPS;

implements UFCPS;

performs experiments with UFCPS;

creates a compatible implementation;

modifies an implementation;

publishes research concerning UFCPS.

5. No Royalty

The UFCPS licensing architecture contains no royalty mechanism.

In particular:

No transaction → no royalty.

More precisely, the final license should not create a royalty obligation at all.

There is no:

per-transaction royalty;

usage fee;

protocol fee;

mandatory payment to the author;

payment for access to the framework;

payment for implementation of the protocol.

Economic mechanisms that may exist inside a future UFCPS research system are separate from the intellectual-property license.

6. Software Implementations

UFCPS implementations should be licensed separately from the conceptual and protocol documentation where technically appropriate.

The final project may therefore contain separate licensing notices for:

Documentation / Specification
        │
        ├── UFCPS protocol license
        │
        └── Metamonism attribution

Source Code
        │
        └── Software license

Research Publications
        │
        └── Publication-specific terms

The exact software license remains to be selected.

Candidate families may include permissive open-source licenses, but no final choice is made by this document.

7. Attribution Statement

The following statement is proposed as the canonical project attribution:

Metamonism — ontological foundation of UFCPS.

UFCPS is based on the Metamonist ontological framework developed by Andrii Myshko.

A shorter form may be used where space is limited:

UFCPS — based on the Metamonist ontological framework of Andrii Myshko.

8. Forks and Derivative Implementations

Forks and derivative implementations should remain technically possible.

A derivative project may change:

implementation;

architecture;

algorithms;

experimental methodology;

interface;

resource model;

execution environment.

The existence of a fork does not imply that the fork is an official UFCPS implementation.

The final license should therefore distinguish:

UFCPS as the original protocol;

official UFCPS implementations;

third-party implementations;

derivative or experimental systems.

Attribution should not be phrased in a way that falsely implies endorsement by the original author.

9. Compatibility and Naming

The final licensing and project policy should distinguish copyright/license permissions from naming and endorsement.

A compatible implementation may state that it is:

compatible with UFCPS

without necessarily being:

an official UFCPS implementation.

If the project later adopts a trademark or protected project name, naming and trademark rules should be documented separately from the copyright license.

10. Separation from Economic Architecture

UFCPS may contain economic experiments, resource accounting, compute compensation, or other economic mechanisms.

Those mechanisms are part of the research architecture and must not automatically be interpreted as licensing charges.

The distinction is:

License
    │
    └── governs rights to use protected project materials

UFCPS Economic Layer
    │
    └── governs experimental economic processes inside a UFCPS system

The two layers should remain legally and technically separate.

11. Open Research Principle

The project should preserve the ability of independent researchers to:

reproduce experiments;

inspect specifications;

implement alternative versions;

challenge assumptions;

report failures;

propose competing models;

extend the framework;

publish independent results.

The licensing architecture should not make agreement with Metamonist philosophical claims a prerequisite for scientific investigation of UFCPS.

A researcher may use, test, criticize, modify, or reject the framework while preserving the required attribution to its documented intellectual origin.

12. Final License Selection

This document does not yet select a final legal license.

Before publishing a final LICENSE file, the project should separately determine:

license for source code;

license for protocol specifications and documentation;

attribution requirements;

treatment of derivative implementations;

naming and trademark policy, if any;

jurisdictional considerations;

compatibility between selected licenses.

The final legal text should be reviewed for the jurisdictions in which the project is intended to operate.

14. Design Principle

The licensing architecture should be consistent with the epistemic character of UFCPS.

The project is intended to be a research program rather than a closed doctrine.

Therefore:

The framework should remain open to investigation while preserving attribution of its intellectual origin.

The license should protect the integrity of authorship without turning an ontological hypothesis into a proprietary barrier to research.
