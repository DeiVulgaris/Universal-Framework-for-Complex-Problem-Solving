UFCPS replaces the demand for immediate local resolution with a mathematical guarantee of **Step Continuity** across a distributed cognitive continuum.

* **Sub-task 2: Swarm Delegation (Absolute Action):** Formalizing structural deadlocks and externalizing metadata into the shared environment to guarantee systemic continuity across distributed matrices.
